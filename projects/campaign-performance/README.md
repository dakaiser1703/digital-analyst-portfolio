# Digital Campaign Performance Dashboard

**Role simulated:** Digital Analyst  
**Dataset:** Synthetic portfolio dataset — not employer/client data.

## Business Question
Which channels generate the strongest combination of reach, engagement, conversion efficiency, and return on advertising spend?

## KPIs
Impressions · Clicks · CTR · Conversions · Conversion Rate · Spend · CPA · Revenue · ROAS

## Findings
- Highest ROAS channel: **Organic Social**
- Highest conversion-rate channel: **Display**
- Overall portfolio ROAS: **3.26x**

## Recommendation
Evaluate media using ROAS and CPA together rather than clicks alone. Protect spend in efficient high-intent channels, test creative and targeting changes in lower-converting channels, and validate incremental lift before major budget shifts.

Digital analytics, marketing analytics, performance marketing, KPI reporting, campaign optimization, conversion analysis, paid media analytics, ROI/ROAS, CPA, funnel performance, data storytelling, stakeholder recommendations.
