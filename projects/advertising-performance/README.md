# Advertising Creative & Audience Performance

**Role simulated:** Digital Advertising Analyst  
**Dataset:** Synthetic portfolio dataset.

## Business Question
Which creative and audience combination is most efficient at driving conversions?

## Finding
Lowest CPA combination: **Video × Lookalike**.

## Recommendation
Scale efficient combinations carefully, maintain structured creative testing, and separate upper-funnel engagement goals from lower-funnel conversion goals.
