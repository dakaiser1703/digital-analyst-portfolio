01 / ABOUT

Curious about
why things work.

I studied Psychology and Dance Performance at Southern Methodist University and minored in Marketing and Advertising. On paper, those subjects look pretty far apart. In practice, they all taught me to pay close attention to people.

Psychology and research taught me how to ask a useful question, work through evidence, notice patterns, and avoid jumping to the first conclusion. Marketing and advertising gave me a way to apply that thinking to audiences, messages, brands, and behavior. Dance trained a different part of the same skill set: preparation, timing, visual awareness, consistency, taking notes, and making adjustments quickly when something is not landing.

That combination is why digital work interests me. A campaign can be creative and still need careful execution. A report can be accurate and still need to make sense to someone who does not live in a spreadsheet. Good copy has to sound like a person wrote it. And when the data shows that something is not working, somebody has to notice, say so, and help figure out what to try next.
